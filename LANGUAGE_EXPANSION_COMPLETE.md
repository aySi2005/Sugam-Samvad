### Examples

Find Russian participants:
```sql
SELECT * FROM participants WHERE participant_language = 'ru';
```

Count German speakers in session:
```sql
SELECT COUNT(*) FROM participants 
WHERE interpretation_session_id = 1 
AND participant_language = 'de';
```

Export by language:
```sql
SELECT * FROM interpretations
```
